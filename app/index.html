    Starts from your first point, or the middle of the map if there isn't one.               Interval stretches
              At least min,

              Stretches of this route with no traffic lights, stop or give way signs, roundabouts, barriers or sharp turns. Crossings count when you're on a path.

                Road safety

                Stretches on main roads (primary, secondary, trunk) or roads signed 80 km/h or more, as mapped in OpenStreetMap. Tap one to see it. To mark them on the map, set Map › Colour route by to "road safety".

                  Roads I like & places to avoid

                  Tap a button, then tap the map. A road is marked up to about 2 km either way from where you tap.

                  Avoided roads are dodged unless the way around is much longer (a 200 m detour round Roe Street: avoided; a 7 km detour round the Narrows: still used). Avoided areas are circles: "avoid completely" never enters; "prefer to avoid" goes around unless the detour is more than about ten times the distance inside. Liked roads can't pull the router onto them, but the loop generator favours them and you'll see how much of each route uses them. All of these are saved on the server, so they apply on every device.
