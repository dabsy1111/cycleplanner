    Route options
      No-go areas

      Circles the router stays out of, like a nasty intersection or a road you hate. "Avoid completely" never enters; "prefer to avoid" goes around unless the detour is more than about ten times the distance inside the circle. Saved on the server, so they apply on every device.

      Size
        Roads I like & dislike

        Tap a button, then tap a road on the map. It marks that road up to about 2 km either way. Disliked roads are avoided unless the way around is much longer (a 200 m detour round Roe Street: avoided; a 7 km detour round the Narrows: still used). Liked roads can't pull the router onto them, but the loop generator favours them and snaps loop points onto them, and you'll see how much of each route uses them.
